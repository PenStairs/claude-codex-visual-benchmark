# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a rigorous and welcoming place for people comparing models, providers, and tools.

## Our standards

Be respectful, evidence-oriented, and specific. Critique a result or methodology without attacking the contributor, model team, provider, or user. Disclose conflicts of interest, sponsored access, custom settings, and failed runs that materially affect a claim. Do not manipulate attribution, fabricate benchmark evidence, publish private data, or use the project to harass individuals.

Unacceptable behavior includes discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, and knowingly misleading benchmark claims.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub Security Advisory when privacy is required, or contact repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or ban repeat offenders. Enforcement decisions should protect affected people and preserve evidence without unnecessarily exposing private details.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
